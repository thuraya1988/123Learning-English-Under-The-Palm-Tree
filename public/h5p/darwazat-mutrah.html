How to Play
صفية وثريا تشكّلان البوابة...
Safiya and Thuraya stand facing each other, holding hands raised high — they are The Gate.
والبنات يمرّون تحت الدروازة وهنّ يغنّين...
The other girls walk under the gate while everyone sings: "Darwazat Mutrah, Darwazat Mutrah..."
وفجأة... تنزل الدروازة!
Suddenly — the gate falls! Whoever gets trapped must answer an English question to escape.
