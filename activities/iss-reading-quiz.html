🛰️ Life on the ISS — True or False?

Unit 3: Let's Explore! · Lesson 7 · Reading Skills

Read first:

The International Space Station (ISS) travels around the Earth at about 28,000 kilometres per hour. Because it moves so fast, astronauts on the ISS see the sun rise and set 16 times every day! There are usually 6 astronauts living and working on the ISS at one time. Every astronaut needs to exercise for about two hours a day, because their muscles get weaker in space. Astronauts eat three meals a day, and all their food is kept in special bags so it doesn't float away!
