🏳️ Our National Symbols

Learn about them
🇴🇲

The Omani Flag

White, red and green — symbolising peace, sacrifice and the fertility of our land, with the national emblem at its centre.

⚔️

The National Emblem

The Omani khanjar (curved dagger) crossed with two swords — a symbol of our history, pride and strength.

🎵

The National Anthem

"Ya Rab Ehfadh Lana Jalalat as-Sultan" — we sing it with pride on every national occasion.

👑

His Majesty the Sultan

His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tariq — may God protect him — the leader of Oman's renewed renaissance.

📅 Our National Days

🇴🇲National Day18 November

Oman's glorious National Day — celebrating the blessed renaissance and the nation's development achievements.

🇴🇲Renaissance Day23 July

Marking the start of Oman's comprehensive renaissance in 1970, which transformed the face of the country.

👩Omani Women's Day17 October

Honouring the role of Omani women and their contributions to the nation's progress since 2009.

🎖️Armed Forces Day11 December

Celebrating the Sultan's Armed Forces and their noble role in protecting the homeland.

🚓Royal Oman Police Day5 January

Honouring the Royal Oman Police for maintaining security, order and road safety.

🌊Omani Environment Day8 January

Raising awareness about protecting Oman's natural resources and unique biodiversity.
