Review & Self-Assessment

Research Checklist

  • I found at least 5 key facts about my topic
  • I used information from the story
  • I wrote my notes in my own words
  • I answered my research questions
  • I thought of new questions to ask

Creation Checklist

  • My title is clear and informative
  • I included at least 4 key facts
  • My conclusion explains what I learned
  • My work is organised and easy to understand
  • I used correct English spelling and grammar
