How to Play

Complete the missing words, then read the story aloud.

Choose a Level

  • Level 1 — Simple Present: Fill in present tense verbs. Great for beginners.
  • Level 2 — Simple Past: Fill in past tense verbs. Includes regular and irregular forms.
  • Level 3 — Mixed Tenses: Past, present, and future in one story. A real challenge.

Fill in the Blanks

  • Each story has 5 to 7 blanks — missing verbs.
  • Click a word from the Word Bank to fill a blank.
  • Or type directly into the blank field.
  • Read the full passage to understand the context before choosing.
  • Each blank has a grammar hint — hover or look below the blank.

Check Your Answers

  • Press Check when you are ready.
  • Correct answers turn green.
  • Wrong answers shake and then show the correct word.
  • After checking, the Arabic translation appears below the passage.
  • Press Next Story to continue.

Scoring

  • 15 points per correct blank.
  • 20 bonus points for a perfect story (all blanks correct).
  • Complete all 4 stories in a level to see your final score.
  • After finishing, read the story aloud to practise speaking.

Tips

  • Read the whole passage first before filling any blanks.
  • Look for time words (every day, yesterday, tomorrow) to choose the right tense.
  • After "can", always use the base verb (run, not runs).
  • Irregular verbs must be memorised — the hints will help.
  • After completing, read the story aloud for speaking practice.
