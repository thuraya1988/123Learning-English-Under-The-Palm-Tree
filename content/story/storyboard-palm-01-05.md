# ستوري بورد الرواية — تحت ظل النخلة
## Storyboard Reference — Under the Palm Tree

**دليل مرجعي لكل مشهد في الرواية**، مستخرج مباشرة من نص الرواية (ما فيه أي إضافة أو اختراع) — لكل مشهد تقسيم لوحات (Panels)، كل لوحة فيها:
- **وصف بصري** للمخرج/الرسام: المكان، الشخصيات الموجودة، الحركة والتعبير.
- **اقتباس/تعليق مختصر** تحت كل لوحة (من نص الرواية نفسه أو تلخيص قصير جداً له).

هذا الجزء يغطي **النخلة الأولى إلى الخامسة (Palm 1–5)** كنموذج أولي — 36 نخلة بالمجمل بانتظار المراجعة والموافقة قبل إكمال الباقي.

## Palm One — Thuraya's Book of Thoughts

### Scene 1 — Turkey / The Notebook

**Panel 1** — Wide establishing shot: a quiet room overlooking the Bosphorus at dawn. Thuraya sits upright on a wooden chair by an open window, hands still, gaze distant. A cup of Turkish coffee sits untouched on the table beside her.
> "The morning had settled over the city like a held breath."

**Panel 2** — Close-up on Thuraya's hand holding a blue Cartier pen, poised but not writing.
> "In her hand, a blue Cartier pen. Her father's gift."

**Panel 3** — Flashback inset: young Thuraya's small hand disappearing inside her father's two hands — Professor Mohammed Al-Naabi, silent, solemn, the day she received her scholarship.
> "He had simply taken her hand in both of his. Just held it."

**Panel 4** — Close-up: worn leather notebook cover, her own handwriting visible on it.
> "'A story that might have been in Thuraya's mind.'"

**Panel 5** — Thuraya opens the notebook, pen raised above the blank page, suspended, not yet writing.
> "She raised the pen above the page. Did not write yet."

**Panel 6** — She begins to write; Arabic script filling the page, her expression calm and focused.
> "Today is my birthday. I am thirty-eight, studying my Master's in Turkey."

**Panel 7** — Pen pauses mid-page, tip barely touching paper, her expression caught between thoughts.
> "Today... I had a strange dream."

**Panel 8** — Thuraya's eyes lift from the page, staring past the window into some unseen distance, fingers tightening on the pen.
> "A dream that took me back to a distant time... How could that year feel this close?"

**Panel 9** — Her hand turns the page slowly; the room around her softens at the edges like a painting left in the sun, then fades to white.
> "And just like that, the room in Turkey grew quiet in a different way — and then it was gone."

---

### Scene 2 — Transition

**Panel 1** — Close on Thuraya's pen slowing on the page, her gaze unfocused, drifting past the notebook.
> "How can that year feel this close?"

**Panel 2** — Thuraya's face in soft stillness, eyes open but turned inward, no longer seeing the Bosphorus outside.
> "Her eyes were open and yet entirely elsewhere. Turned inward. Turned backward."

**Panel 3** — Her thumb at the edge of the notebook page, turning it with ceremonial care, as if crossing a threshold.
> "As though turning it was less an act of reading than an act of crossing."

**Panel 4** — The room subtly shifts — curtains, coffee cup, and window light all seem to draw back like a tide going out.
> "The room was no longer the same room. The morning was no longer the same morning."

**Panel 5** — Thuraya lowers the pen to the page with sudden certainty; a number and a word appear on the white paper.
> "The number sat alone on the white page — stark, patient, absolute."

**Panel 6** — Wide shot: Thuraya motionless in her chair as the whole scene dissolves around her.
> "Without moving from her chair, she was no longer in Turkey at all."

### Scene 3 — London Apartment

**Panel 1** — Establishing shot: grey London skyline, flat pale sky pressed over rooftops, cold and permanent.
> "The sky over Britain was the colour of old linen."

**Panel 2** — Interior: Sophia curled into a wooden chair by the window, knees drawn up, deeply absorbed in a book titled *The Palm Tree*.
> "She was entirely absorbed in her book."

**Panel 3** — Close-up: a small table beside her — a closed, unlit box of Omani frankincense, untouched for weeks.
> "Omani frankincense. Closed. Unlit. Untouched."

**Panel 4** — Close-up: a framed photograph beside the box — a young boy standing between his parents, mountains and palm trees behind them.
> "John. Behind them — mountains. Palm trees. A place that was entirely not here."

**Panel 5** — Sophia's fingers go still on the page; she lifts her head slightly, sensing something in the air.
> "A scent. Arriving before he did. Oud."

**Panel 6** — Sophia turns in her chair to look toward the doorway, having taken one breath first.
> "She breathed in first. Then turned."

**Panel 7** — John standing just inside the room, still, watching her, an open letter in his hand.
> "A letter was in his hand. Open. Read."

**Panel 8** — Two-shot: Sophia and John locking eyes across the room in charged silence.
> "And in that silence, before a single word had been spoken — she already knew."

### Scene 4 — The Decision

**Panel 1** — John standing near Sophia's chair, voice low; Sophia's eyes still on her book, not really reading.
> "'Sophia…'"

**Panel 2** — Sophia looks up, sets the book down deliberately, gives him full attention.
> "'You sound serious.'"

**Panel 3** — John steps closer, letter held carefully in hand.
> "'I got the job.'"

**Panel 4** — Close-up: Sophia's fingers tightening on the spine of the book.
> "'What job?'"

**Panel 5** — John, steady and calm, meeting her eyes.
> "'In Oman.'"

**Panel 6** — Sophia, puzzled, processing the word.
> "'Oman… as in the Middle East?' — 'Yes.'"

**Panel 7** — John speaking plainly, listing facts one by one.
> "'They are opening schools. Building roads. Starting something new. And they asked for me.'"

**Panel 8** — Sophia sits down slowly, overwhelmed, needing ground beneath her.
> "'I don't know anything about Oman.'"

**Panel 9** — John, honest and unguarded, no false confidence.
> "'I know only a little… but I know this — it's a chance. A real chance.'"

**Panel 10** — Sophia studying his face intently, then her eyes drifting toward the table — frankincense box, photograph, mountains, palms.
> "Her eyes moved — away from him, toward the table."

**Panel 11** — Sophia's face goes distant, the room blurring around her as memory pulls her under.
> "Without crossing any distance — she was no longer in the room."

### Scene 5 — Memory / University

**Panel 1** — Wide shot: a large university lecture hall, students seated in rows with notebooks open, afternoon light in long diagonals. On the board: "Literature Around the World."
> "A lecture hall. Wide and high-ceilinged."

**Panel 2** — The professor, an Iraqi man with commanding presence, teaching at the front of the hall.
> "He did not simply teach. He inhabited the material."

**Panel 3** — Sophia seated mid-row, pen moving slower than the others, pausing.
> "She was not writing. She was listening to something the lecture had not put into the room."

**Panel 4** — Sophia's pen stops; her head lifts toward a voice speaking fluent Arabic from the side of the room.
> "A voice. Off to the side. Speaking in a language she did not know."

**Panel 5** — John standing at the side of the lecture hall, speaking fluent Arabic with total ease, addressing the professor.
> "Not reading from anything, speaking fluently — as though the language was already part of the body."

**Panel 6** — The professor responding warmly; other students glancing up, some confused, some impressed, most returning to their notes.
> "The exchange moved between them with the effortless rhythm of two people sharing the same native water."

**Panel 7** — Close on Sophia, leaning back slightly, watching John intently — not the words, but the person.
> "Who is he?"

**Panel 8** — Sophia looking down at her notebook, staring at an unfinished sentence, distracted.
> "She did not finish it. She was thinking about something else entirely."

### Scene 6 — Back to Present / The Choice

**Panel 1** — Sophia blinks; the lecture hall dissolves like smoke around her.
> "She blinked. The lecture hall dissolved."

**Panel 2** — Back in the London apartment — John still standing exactly where she left him, holding the letter, waiting patiently.
> "John had not moved. Still holding the letter, still patient, still waiting."

**Panel 3** — Sophia studies his face carefully, the same searching look from the lecture hall years before.
> "The way she had looked at him in that lecture hall years ago, for the first time."

**Panel 4** — Her gaze drifts again to the table — frankincense, photograph, the boy, the mountains, the palms.
> "A place that had always been part of him — long before she was."

**Panel 5** — Sophia closes her book softly and sets it down on the table with deliberate finality.
> "The sound it made — soft, final — seemed to mark something."

**Panel 6** — Sophia, calm and direct, looking straight at John.
> "'I'm coming with you.'"

**Panel 7** — John frozen, stunned, absorbing what she's said.
> "'You're not even going to argue?'"

**Panel 8** — Sophia shakes her head gently.
> "'No.' — 'Book the tickets.'"

**Panel 9** — John exhales, shoulders dropping in visible relief, almost to himself.
> "'Together…'"

### Scene 7 — Preparing

**Panel 1** — A first suitcase appears by the apartment door, then a second, then a third.
> "First one bag appeared near the door. Then a second. Then a third."

**Panel 2** — Wide shot: the apartment overrun with bags — stacked, leaning, covering every surface and the floor.
> "There was no longer a floor to speak of."

**Panel 3** — John standing frozen in the middle of the chaos, staring around him in disbelief.
> "'Sophia… are we moving to Oman… or relocating London?'"

**Panel 4** — Sophia on the floor beside an open suitcase, calmly and precisely folding clothes.
> "'This is normal.' — 'For women.'"

**Panel 5** — John picking up a dress from a pile, turning it over in his hands, incredulous.
> "'…You told me you have nothing to wear.'"

**Panel 6** — John's small, defeated, involuntary smile as he sets the dress down and shakes his head.
> "A quiet smile pulled at the corner of his mouth — the smile of a man who has lost a battle."

**Panel 7** — John crouching beside an unopened suitcase, unzipping it, beginning to help pack; Sophia glances over with a small private smile.
> "Without argument, he crouched beside the nearest suitcase — and began to help."

### Scene 8 — The Flight

**Panel 1** — The airplane climbing steeply, London's grey rooftops dissolving into cloud below the window.
> "Britain fell away beneath it — the grey rooftops dissolving into grey cloud."

**Panel 2** — Sophia at the window seat, palm pressed lightly to the cold glass, watching the last of the city vanish.
> "She pressed her hand lightly to the cold glass. As if touching it were a kind of farewell."

**Panel 3** — John asleep beside her, head tilted, face unguarded and peaceful.
> "John was asleep. Not lightly — completely."

**Panel 4** — Sophia alert and awake, hands resting in her lap, staring out at endless white cloud.
> "Awake and still, her attention entirely outward."

**Panel 5** — The clouds break apart outside the window, revealing dark, dramatic mountain shapes below.
> "Mountains. Wide and dry and absolute."

**Panel 6** — Sophia leaning close to the glass, startled — the terrain below is rugged and immense, not the flat sand she expected.
> "What she was looking at was terrain of enormous presence, of density and weight."

**Panel 7** — A shaft of sharp, direct sunlight crosses the cabin and touches Sophia's face through the window.
> "The light arrived directly — clean, intense, making everything it touched more sharply itself."

**Panel 8** — Sophia glancing sideways at sleeping John, half-whispering, mountains still visible below.
> "'How…?' she whispered. Not expecting an answer."

**Panel 9** — Sophia pressing her forehead to the glass, gazing down at the mountains in quiet realization.
> "This was a life. Beginning now. Nothing after this would be the same."

### Scene 9 — Arrival in Oman

**Panel 1** — The airplane door swings open; visible heat shimmer rolls into the cabin doorway.
> "The airplane door opened — and the heat did not wait."

**Panel 2** — Sophia frozen mid-step at the top of the boarding stairs, fanning herself with a boarding pass, squinting in the sun.
> "'This is not warm.' — 'This is fire.'"

**Panel 3** — John descending the stairs behind her, hand raised to shield his eyes from the glare.
> "He lifted his hand against the sun instinctively, squinting into the brightness."

**Panel 4** — Close on John's face — a flicker of old, wordless recognition as he looks out at the tarmac and low buildings.
> "'I don't remember it being this hot… when I was a child. Or maybe… I forgot.'"

**Panel 5** — Wide shot: heat shimmering visibly off the tarmac in waves, the two of them small against the bright, open airfield.
> "'How do people live in this?'"

**Panel 6** — Interior: a small, quiet airport terminal, few people, no rush, everyone moving unhurried.
> "The airport was smaller than she had imagined. Quieter."

**Panel 7** — Sophia gripping her bag strap tightly, uneasy, looking to John.
> "'What if we get lost?'"

**Panel 8** — John scanning overhead signs calmly, pointing the way forward.
> "'Exit.' 'Taxi.' — 'We'll take it step by step.'"

**Panel 1** — John standing at the luggage belt, leaning forward, watching bags circle past.

**Panel 2** — A growing pile of suitcases stacking up beside him — one, two, five, eight, ten.

**Panel 3** — John muttering under his breath, gesturing at the mountain of luggage, half-smiling.

**Panel 4** — Sophia, ignoring him completely, her gaze fixed on something across the arrivals hall.

**Panel 5** — Across the hall: a composed woman in a neat hijab standing perfectly still, waiting, raising her hand in a single unhurried wave.

**Panel 6** — Close on Sophia's face, recognition dawning slowly.
> "'That's her…'"

**Panel 7** — Sophia walking briskly through the crowd toward the woman, John turning to follow.
> "Sophia was already moving, walking through the crowd with certainty."

**Panel 1** — Sophia and John approaching Thuraya across the thinning crowd of the arrivals hall.

**Panel 6** — Thuraya nodding back warmly.

## Palm Two — The First Walk in the Village

### Scene 1 — The First Night

**Panel 1** — Interior, night. A small clay-walled room lit by a trembling qandeel lamp, a thin bisat on the floor, no bed. Sophia stands near the open window, one hand resting lightly on the wooden frame. John sleeps in the background.
> "The room did not announce itself — quiet, waiting to be lived in."

**Panel 2** — Close on Sophia's face at the window, eyes half-closed, breathing in the night air layered with scent.
> "Oud first. Then musk. Then — Luban."

**Panel 3** — Sophia glances back at John, who sleeps entirely, without resistance; a faint smile pulls at the corner of her mouth.
> "Behind her, John was asleep — entirely, without resistance."

**Panel 4** — Wide shot from the window: dark village rooftops below, a distant minaret against the pre-dawn sky.
> "The adhan for Fajr rose — deepening the quiet, not breaking it."

**Panel 5** — Sophia leans forward, eyes searching the path below; small running figures appear in the dim light.
> "Then — movement. Footsteps, faster now. Children."

**Panel 6** — Low-angle view of children running down the dark path toward the mosque, urgent and joyful.
> "'يالله يالله صلااااه!' — 'أنا أسبق! أنا أسبق!'"

**Panel 7** — Sophia watching, thoughtful, the path now empty again.
> "If they wake before the sun does — if they move through the dark this willingly—"

**Panel 8** — Sophia turns back toward sleeping John, mouth parting as if to speak, then closing it.
> "She almost said something. She didn't."

**Panel 9** — Sophia murmuring quietly to herself at the window, alone with the thought.
> "'Where are the girls?' she murmured. 'And the women?'"

**Panel 10** — Sophia sinks down and sits against the clay wall, eyes closing, sleep finally taking her.
> "Tomorrow — she would walk this place."

### Scene 2 — Letj

**Panel 1** — Bright morning, sun already high over the village path. Thuraya stands waiting, still and at ease.
> "'Ready?' Sophia nodded. 'Yes.'"

**Panel 2** — Sophia, John, and Thuraya walking together along an uneven earthen path between close-set clay houses.
> "The houses stood close to the land — not placed upon it, but growing from it."

**Panel 3** — Sophia looking around at the open, quiet village as they walk.
> "'Everything feels very open,' Sophia said. 'And very quiet.'"

**Panel 4** — John gesturing as he speaks; Thuraya glances at him, approving.
> "'It's like the village is breathing slowly,' John said."

**Panel 5** — A group of girls in a loose circle at the path's edge; one throws a stone into rectangles drawn in the dirt.
> "'This is letj,' Thuraya said."

**Panel 6** — Close on a girl hopping precisely through the drawn squares, focused and practiced, avoiding every line.
> "One foot in the single squares, both feet apart in the paired ones."

**Panel 7** — Sophia watching intently, exhaling as if she'd been holding her breath.
> "'That's not easy.' 'No,' Thuraya said. 'It isn't.'"

**Panel 8** — Sophia steps forward and takes the stone from a girl, studying the ground; John smiles beside her.
> "'I want to try.' John: 'Of course you do.'"

**Panel 9** — Sophia mid-hop, her foot landing right on the line; the girls around her burst into laughter.
> "Her foot found the edge of the line. She stopped. 'I lost.'"

**Panel 10** — Malood the goat comes charging through the game, scattering the drawn lines; girls shout and chase after him.
> "'هيييه! مالود!' — furious and delighted in equal measure."

**Panel 11** — The girls crouch back down, redrawing the shapes in the dust with their fingers, unbothered.
> "No debate. No decision required. They simply returned to it."

### Scene 3 — The Falaj

**Panel 1** — The group walking; ahead, a narrow stone water channel comes into view, the sound of running water rising.
> "'Do you hear that?' Sophia slowed. John nodded. 'Yes.'"

**Panel 2** — Close on the falaj: clear water flowing through a carved stone channel, light catching its surface in shifting patterns.
> "It did not rush. It did not pause. It simply flowed."

**Panel 3** — Wide shot along the falaj: women washing clothes and scrubbing large pots at its edges, children splashing in the water.
> "Women washed clothes. Children played in the same water."

**Panel 4** — Malood lowers his head and drinks from the channel, unbothered, right beside the splashing children.
> "And just to the side — Malood. He drank. Without consulting anyone."

**Panel 5** — Sophia and John standing at the edge; Sophia's expression is astonished as she takes it all in.
> "'They are using the same water for everything.'"

**Panel 6** — Across the channel, Nasser pours water from the falaj over the pickup truck in long arcs, scrubbing it clean.
> "Across the channel, Nasser poured water over the truck in long arcs."

**Panel 7** — John crouches at the water's edge and splashes his face with both hands, looking up refreshed.
> "He looked up — entirely refreshed, entirely unconcerned."

**Panel 8** — Sophia, hands raised in disbelief, staring at John.
> "'John! You just washed your face in that!' 'Yes.'"

**Panel 9** — Two women by the water notice Sophia and John, smiling and speaking to each other, one laughing.
> "'They're talking about us, aren't they?' 'Yes.'"

**Panel 10** — Sophia's eyes widen; Thuraya smiles beside her, amused.
> "'They are asking who is your husband.' 'Already?'"

**Panel 11** — Sophia gives a small, uncertain wave to the women, who wave back warmly; her expression settles into quiet thought as she looks back at the water.
> "The same water. Different uses. She was beginning to understand."

### Scene 4 — Sadoo's Shop

**Panel 1** — John and Sophia walking on; John glances back the way they came, mid-conversation about the falaj.
> "'It's still there. Like a long line running through everything.'"

**Panel 2** — John mid-stride is suddenly knocked off balance by something unseen from the side, toppling toward the water.
> "Something hit him. Fast. Entirely without warning."

**Panel 3** — John sits soaked in the falaj, stunned; Thuraya and Sophia laugh above him; Malood stands at the edge looking down placidly.
> "The water received him with cold, democratic completeness."

**Panel 4** — John stands up dripping; Malood presses close, following him persistently.
> "'Now you have two [friends].' 'The falaj… and Malood.'"

**Panel 5** — A small open-fronted shop comes into view around a bend in the path, simple and well-worn.
> "'This is Sadoo's shop,' Thuraya said."

**Panel 6** — Children swarm toward the shop from every direction, shouting and reaching.
> "'Sadووو!' — 'أنا باغي مينو!' — 'أنا باغي داغوص!'"

**Panel 7** — Inside the shop, Sadoo calmly hands out snacks amid the crowd of children, composed and efficient.
> "Sadoo moved between them — all of this is simply Tuesday."

**Panel 8** — A barefoot, dusty boy proudly dips his snack into spicy daghoos sauce.
> "'Children eat spicy food?' 'Yes. They start early.'"

**Panel 9** — Sophia holds up a colorful boba bottle, reading the label and shaking it curiously; John watches, amused.
> "'Bo… ba?' 'A drink.' 'It's gas?'"

**Panel 10** — Malood bursts into the shop, jumping onto boxes and sniffing shelves; children chase him back out in chaos.
> "'يالله! مالود! برا!' — half the village now involved."

**Panel 11** — Sophia laughs so hard she has to hold the doorframe; John, still wet, stands beside her with Malood nearby.
> "Sophia laughed so hard she had to hold the door."

**Panel 12** — Across the road, Saeed stands alone and still, watching everything without joining in; Thuraya's expression softens as she notices him.
> "'Who is that boy?' 'That is Saeed. He is not allowed to go to school.'"

### Scene 5 — Thursday / Khamis

**Panel 1** — The path ahead; Sophia walks slightly slower than before, taking things in more fully.
> "She was watching differently now. With more of herself in it."

**Panel 2** — A tall man walks ahead of them in a simple dishdasha and tightly wound turban, carrying a ma'ool with a worn metal edge.
> "Tall. Firm posture. A ma'ool in his hand, its edge darkened from years of use."

**Panel 3** — Behind the man, a boy walks in the exact same rhythm — same pace, same weight, same angle in the shoulders.
> "Behind him — a boy. Same pace, same weight, same angle in the shoulders."

**Panel 4** — Sophia asks Thuraya quietly who they are; Thuraya answers simply.
> "'Who is that?' 'That is Khamis.'"

**Panel 5** — John stops, surprised, turning to Thuraya.
> "'Like… Thursday? You name people after days of the week?'"

**Panel 6** — Thuraya explains with an amused smile; John laughs.
> "'If the child is born on Thursday, he can be called Khamis.'"

**Panel 7** — Close on the boy Khamis walking behind his father, entirely absorbed in the rhythm, unaware of being watched.
> "'This doesn't feel like a child,' Sophia said. 'He looks older.'"

**Panel 8** — Wide shot: the man and boy recede into the distance down the path; Sophia watches them go.
> "'I want to understand.' Thuraya: 'You will.'"

### Scene 6 — Nahsher / Another World

**Panel 1** — The path leads into a greener, shaded grove; trees heavy with clusters of colorful fruit.
> "'Look!' Trees — farsadh and lamba, Thuraya explained."

**Panel 2** — A group of boys under the trees; one throws a stone up into the branches, fruit and leaves falling.
> "They are doing nahsher. They throw stones so the fruit falls."

**Panel 3** — Other boys run forward, quickly collecting the fallen fruit in a well-practiced system.
> "No wasted movement. A system arrived at through practice."

**Panel 4** — John repeats the new word carefully; Sophia smiles at him; Thuraya laughs.
> "'Nah… sher.' 'Let's go and… nahsher.'"

**Panel 5** — Nearby, boys eat fruit dipped in something bright orange — salt, pepper, and chili.
> "'They put salt and pepper… on mango?' 'And chili.'"

**Panel 6** — John reaches forward and takes a small piece of the spiced mango; Sophia watches, skeptical.
> "Because he was the kind of man who believed in trying things."

**Panel 7** — Close on John's face mid-chew, working through the flavor, then breaking into a slow smile.
> "'It's… strange. But good.'"

**Panel 8** — Wide shot, sky turning orange; the family walks back toward the apartment as the village settles into evening — Sadoo closing his shop, children heading home.
> "The village settled around them as the afternoon folded into evening."

**Panel 9** — In the distance, Saeed still stands unmoved, watching quietly as the light fades.
> "Still silent. Still thinking. Dreaming of a place called school."

**Panel 10** — Interior, night. Sophia sits on the bisat, looking around the clay-walled room, then at John.
> "'John… I think we didn't move to another country.'"

**Panel 11** — John looks at her, questioning; Sophia smiles.
> "'Where did we move?' She smiled. 'We moved to another world.'"

## Palm Three — The First Day at School

### Scene 1 — The Assembly Yard

**Panel 1** — Establishing shot: a small mud-brick village school, stone and clay walls, two long classrooms, a wooden door, a small dusty yard out front. Morning light.
> "The school did not announce itself."

**Panel 2** — Interior classroom: worn wooden desks carved with children's names, a not-very-black blackboard, chalk. Empty, waiting.
> "For the children of Al-Qurawashiyah, this place was something new."

**Panel 3** — Wide village shot: mothers walking children to school, some boys running ahead, some girls walking slowly clutching books like treasures.
> "That morning, the village moved differently."

**Panel 4** — Inside the classroom, Thuraya alone at the board, arranging chalk, wiping the table with her hand, listening to distant footsteps and noise, smiling to herself.
> "They are coming."

**Panel 5** — John stands near the door holding a book like a soldier with a map; Sophia beside him in a simple long dress and light headscarf, looking around nervously.
> "I feel like I am about to meet one hundred small judges."

**Panel 6** — John smiling reassuringly at Sophia.
> "They are not judges. They are students." / "They look dangerous."

**Panel 7** — The teachers' room: wide blue wooden doors, a bisat flat on the floor, a slow ceiling fan, wooden tables, shelves of books in disorder. Sophia moving through it slowly, John running his hand along a table edge.
> "It's different." / "It's enough."

**Panel 8** — The empty classroom itself: stone walls, one blue wooden door, a single unfiltered window, wooden benches. Sophia standing alone in the center, looking around.
> "This room was ready. Whether she was, was another question."

**Panel 9** — The assembly yard: wide, flat, sun-hardened ground, a wooden flagpole with a folded cloth at its base. Thuraya, John, and Sophia standing near it.
> "The flag."

**Panel 10** — John and Sophia unfolding the flag together — red, white, and green colors emerging, the emblem catching the light against the dusty clay yard.
> "Against the clay and dust — the flag did not blend. It stood."

**Panel 11** — Children pouring into the yard in a rush, dust rising behind their running feet, then slowing into loose rows.
> "They were coming because they had decided to come."

**Panel 12** — Sophia stepping forward toward a group of boys, Thuraya translating beside her.
> "Why are you late?" — "They had a Qur'an circle before school."

### Scene 2 — The Students

**Panel 1** — Classroom doorway: children flooding in loudly, laughing, pushing, scattering toward seats with no order at all.
> "They came like birds."

**Panel 2** — Children settling under the tree: Mansoor sitting too close to Ahmed, Ahmed shifting away, Mansoor following, Ahmed sighing.

**Panel 3** — John kneels and draws two words in the dust with a stick — "Hello" and "Good morning" — Thuraya standing by, giving a small teacherly nod of approval.
> He drew, in the dust: Hello. Good morning.

**Panel 4** — Children repeating the words in ragged unison — Mansoor too loud, Khalil in a funny voice, Ahmed precise, Mathila whispering to Rahma first to check she has it right.
> "Hello." "Good morning." — each child, in their own way.

**Panel 5** — Noura raises her hand with a thoughtful, deliberate expression, the other children watching her expectantly.
> "Why we say good? Why not just — morning?"

**Panel 6** — John, caught off guard, looks toward Thuraya for help; Thuraya only smiles, offering nothing — the question is his.
> He had never been asked this question before.

**Panel 7** — John admits honestly, in front of all the watching children, that he does not know — Ahmed looking on with quiet alarm at a teacher without an answer.
> "I do not know the answer. But I will tell you what I think."

**Panel 8** — John speaking earnestly to the circle of children under the tree, gesturing gently, sincere.
> "Good morning says: the sun is up, and I am glad you are here."

**Panel 9** — Noura turns to the small boy beside her and says "Good morning" firmly, gift-like; the boy, confused, then smiling, echoes it back in careful English. The children begin passing the phrase around the circle.
> "GOOD MORNING." — a small gift, handed around the circle.

### Scene 5 — Malood Takes the Page

**Panel 1** — Quiet aftermath under the ghaf tree: Safiya writing in her notebook, Thuraya reading over Ahmed's shoulder, John looking pleased with himself, a breeze stirring the dry leaves.
> The morning, having handed over its gifts, settled into its middle hour.

**Panel 2** — Malood the goat trots into frame from the falaj path, eyeing John's bag with sudden, total focus.
> Into all of this — like a small, horned comet — Malood.

**Panel 3** — Malood leaps lightly onto John's bag and seizes a corner of paper in his teeth, already turning to run.
> He seized the corner of a piece of paper in his teeth.

**Panel 4** — Freeze-frame beat: the children and adults frozen mid-motion, stunned, as Malood bolts down the path with the paper flapping from his mouth.
> Nobody moved. Malood was already halfway down the path.

**Panel 5** — John, shouting his goat's name in disbelief, the whole scene erupting into motion — children scrambling up around him.
> "MALOOD."

**Panel 6** — Noura springs up first, declaring she'll catch him, already running; behind her a cascading chase — Khalil, the smaller boys, Mansoor, the girls' cluster, then a reluctant Ahmed closing his exercise book.
> "I will catch him!" — and she was already running.

**Panel 7** — John himself joins the chase at the rear of the ragged line, shouting English words at a goat that has no interest in learning them; Safiya and Thuraya stay behind under the tree, watching and laughing.
> The goat ahead, the children behind, the Englishman at the rear.

**Panel 8** — Ahmed alone, walking calmly in the opposite direction from the chase, unhurried, thinking.
> "I walked in the other direction. Goats always go back to qit."

**Panel 9** — Ahmed returns to the tree first, calmly carrying the recovered (slightly chewed) paper, handing it back to John's place without ceremony, then sitting to resume his exercise book exactly where he left off.
> He handed the paper back, without ceremony, and kept reading.

**Panel 10** — Safiya and Thuraya exchange a look, then question Ahmed together; Ahmed answers simply, without pride, while the rest of the chase returns hot, laughing, and defeated in the background with a second stolen paper story from Sadoo's shop.
> "Will you be a teacher when you are older?" / "I will see."

### Scene 6 — The River That Is Not a River

**Panel 1** — Late afternoon light, long gold lines through the palm fronds. Safiya and John walk slowly home along the falaj path, tired and content; Thuraya has gone her separate way.
> The village, having spent its midday loudly, entered its quieter hour.

**Panel 2** — The pair pass the now-empty spots that held the day's scenes — the women's station, the children's swimming place, the ghaf tree — each quiet and still.
> Each spot now held the faint echo of the hour it had belonged to.

**Panel 3** — Safiya and John sit together on the low stone edge of the falaj channel, close enough to hear and touch the water, the channel quiet, alone with itself.
> "I want to sit here for a moment." / "Here."

**Panel 4** — Close on the water in the channel, calm and continuous, catching the late gold light — a visual montage suggestion of all the day's uses (drinking, wudu, bathing, washing, John's face) layered faintly in the flow.
> Nothing was lost. Nothing was wasted. The water simply circulated.

**Panel 5** — Safiya, thoughtful, turning to John, recalling her first-week judgment about the falaj.
> "Do you remember what I said the first day? 'It cannot be clean.'"

**Panel 6** — Safiya dips one finger into the water and lifts it, watching a single drop fall back, explaining her realization to John.
> "In London, clean means separated. Here, clean means ordered."

**Panel 7** — John looking out at the long channel toward the palms, absorbing what she's said, quiet and serious.
> "It is not dirtier than London. It is cleaner. Differently cleaner."

**Panel 8** — The two of them sitting in silence as the light lowers further, the first far-off notes of the Maghrib call beginning to warm in the distance.
> "We moved to another world," she said.

**Panel 9** — John reaches for Safiya's hand and holds it with both of his, wordless, echoing an earlier memory of her father and the pen.
> He held her hand long enough for something to pass between them.

**Panel 10** — Night interior: their small clay house lit by a single hanging qandeel lamp. Safiya writes three careful sentences in her notebook with her father's Cartier pen; John reads over her shoulder, then adds one line of his own and closes the notebook. In the corner, Malood the goat lies curled and asleep on the bisat, one ear twitching.
> "Tomorrow, we teach again." — and the falaj, outside, continued.
