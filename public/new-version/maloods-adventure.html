MALOOD'S ADVENTURE

A Side-Scrolling Word Collector

DISASTER IN SAMAIL! Hungry Malood ate all the children's English words! The exam is tomorrow. Help Malood collect the lost words before sunrise.

How to Play

1
← → or A D — Move Malood left and right
2
SPACE or ↑ — Jump (hold for higher jump)
3
Collect letter cards in order to spell English words
4
Stomp enemies from above, or avoid them
5
Reach the flag at the end of each level
6
Malood is the white goat with the arrow marker above
